Privacy Policy — Muse WHOOP Connector

Effective: November 9, 2026

This page is the privacy policy for Muse WHOOP Connector, a personal integration that connects the owner's WHOOP account to their personal AI assistant (Muse) for fitness tracking. It is built and used by one person, for their own data.

Data we access

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the app reads the following WHOOP data:

  • Recovery (recovery score, heart rate variability, resting heart rate) — read:recovery
  • Physiological cycles / strain — read:cycles
  • Sleep (duration, stages, performance) — read:sleep
  • Workouts — read:workout

The offline scope is used only to obtain a refresh token so the connection stays authorized without asking the owner to sign in again.

How data is used

WHOOP data is used solely to show the owner their own metrics — recovery, sleep, strain, and workouts — and to support their personal health and fitness goals. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model.

Data storage and retention

WHOOP data is fetched live when the owner asks for it and is not stored in a permanent database. Summaries the owner asks to keep (for example, a weekly weight check-in note) live in the owner's own notes. OAuth tokens are held in Muse's secure credential store and are never written into code, files, or chat.

Your choices

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the WHOOP Developer Dashboard or by deleting the app there. Revoking immediately stops all further data access.

Children's data

This app is for the owner's personal use only and does not collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change with it.
